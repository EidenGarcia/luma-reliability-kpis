About the data

  • Figures are system SAIDI and SAIFI (transmission and distribution) calculated under IEEE 1366. They exclude Major Event Days such as hurricanes, planned outages, and outages caused by generation shortfalls, so customers experienced more outage time than shown.
  • FY2020–FY2023: PREB Resolution and Order of December 21, 2023, case NEPR-MI-2019-0007, Table 1. FY2024: PREB's FY2024 performance resolution. FY2025–FY2026: LUMA's Q4 FY2026 quarterly report as reported by El Vocero (July 2026); these are approximate and may be restated.
  • FY2020 and most of FY2021 were operated by PREPA. LUMA took over on June 1, 2021.
  • CAIDI (average minutes per outage) is calculated here as SAIDI ÷ SAIFI.
  • The exact annual target for each contract year is in PREB's January 26, 2024 order in case NEPR-AP-2020-0025 and is not shown. Filings: energia.pr.gov · NEPR-MI-2019-0007.
